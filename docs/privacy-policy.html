Privacy Policy — Bahá'í Calendar

Effective Date: November 22, 2026
Package ID: eu.kiefte.bahaicalendar
Developer Contact: ajkiefte@gmail.com

Bahá'í Calendar ("the App") is built with privacy first. The App operates locally on your device, requires no account creation, displays no advertisements, contains no analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not transmit your personal data to any developer-controlled server.

Permissions & How Data Is Used

1. Calendar (READ_CALENDAR, WRITE_CALENDAR)

Used strictly on your device to show your Android/Google Calendar appointments alongside Badí' dates, Holy Days, and Feast schedules, and to let you create or edit calendar events from the App and its home screen widgets. All reads and writes go directly to Android's local Calendar Provider. The App never uploads, shares, or sells your calendar data.

2. Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

Used optionally to calculate accurate local sunrise and sunset times (as the Badí' day begins at sunset), fasting times, and the Qiblih compass bearing. Location coordinates are stored only in private local app preferences on your device. You may also choose a preset city manually without granting location permission. When automatic location detection is used, the App calls Android's built-in system Geocoder to display your city and country name.

3. Vibration (VIBRATE)

Used solely for optional haptic feedback (such as the chanting counter).

Network Access & Third-Party SDKs

  • The App does not request the INTERNET permission and makes no direct network calls.
  • The App contains no third-party advertising, analytics, telemetry, or tracking SDKs.

Data Retention & Deletion

All preferences and saved location coordinates remain exclusively on your device. You can delete all app data at any time by clearing storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the App.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
